# Science & Technology — Current Affairs Revision

## Space Missions

### India's Space Programme (ISRO)

#### Chandrayaan-3 (2023)
- **Lander:** Vikram | **Rover:** Pragyan
- **Landing date:** August 23, 2023
- **Landing site:** South Pole of Moon (Shiv Shakti Point)
- **Achievement:** First country to land near lunar south pole
- India = **4th country** to achieve soft lunar landing (after USA, USSR, China)

> **Exam Tip:** August 23 declared **National Space Day** in India.

#### Aditya-L1 (2023)
- India's **first solar mission**
- Placed at **Lagrange Point 1 (L1)** — ~1.5 million km from Earth
- Studies solar corona, solar wind, solar flares
- Launched: **September 2, 2023**

#### PSLV-C60 (2024) — SpaDeX Mission
- Space Docking Experiment
- India becomes **4th country** to demonstrate space docking

#### Gaganyaan Mission
- India's first **human spaceflight mission**
- Crew of **3 astronauts** to LEO (400 km altitude)
- Astronaut-Designates (Vyomnauts): Prashanth Nair, Ajit Krishnan, Angad Pratap, Shubhanshu Shukla

## Quantum Technology

### National Quantum Mission (India)
- **Budget:** ₹6,003 crore over 8 years (2023–2031)
- **Goals:** Quantum computers with 50-1000 physical qubits, secure quantum communication
- **Implementing body:** Department of Science & Technology (DST)

### India's Quantum Milestones
- First **quantum key distribution (QKD)** link: Sanchar Bhawan ↔ NIC, Delhi
- **C-DOT** (Centre for Development of Telematics) leads quantum comms
