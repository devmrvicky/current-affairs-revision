## Important Books by Indian Leaders

### Jawaharlal Nehru
- ***The Discovery of India*** (1946) — Written in Ahmednagar Fort prison; traces Indian history
- ***Glimpses of World History*** — Letters to Indira Gandhi from prison
- ***An Autobiography*** — His political memoirs

### Mahatma Gandhi
- ***The Story of My Experiments with Truth*** — Autobiography (original in Gujarati)
- ***Hind Swaraj*** (1909) — Vision for Indian self-rule
- ***Key to Health*** — Health philosophy

### B.R. Ambedkar
- ***Annihilation of Caste*** (1936) — Critique of caste system
- ***The Buddha and His Dhamma*** — His last major work

## International Literary Awards

### Man Booker Prize (now just "The Booker Prize")
- For best **English-language novel** published in UK
- Prize money: **£50,000**
- 2024 winner: Samantha Harvey for *Orbital*

### International Booker Prize
- For best fiction **translated into English**
- Prize split between author and translator
- 2024: Jenny Erpenbeck's *Kairos*

### Nobel Prize in Literature 2024
- **Han Kang** (South Korea)
- "For her intense poetic prose that confronts historical traumas and exposes the fragility of human life"
- Notable works: *The Vegetarian*, *Human Acts*, *The White Book*

### Pulitzer Prize
- Administered by **Columbia University**
- For American journalism and arts
- 2024 Fiction: *James* by Percival Everett

## Key Terms

- **Pulitzer Prize:** American journalism & arts — Columbia University
- **Booker Prize:** English fiction — UK-based
- **Nobel Literature:** World's most prestigious literary prize
- **Jnanpith:** India's highest — since 1965
- **Sahitya Akademi:** India's national letters academy
- **Padma Shri** can also be given to authors for contribution to literature
