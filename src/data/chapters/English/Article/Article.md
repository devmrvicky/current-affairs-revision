## 1️⃣ Article Family Tree

```
                        ARTICLE
                           │
            ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
      INDEFINITE (a/an)              DEFINITE (the)
            │                              │
   unspecified / one of many      specific, known, already
   singular countable ONLY        mentioned noun
                                   works with singular, plural
                                   AND uncountable nouns
```

---

## 2️⃣ 4 Golden Rules (never break these)

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | Never use a **singular countable noun** alone — always add an article/determiner |
| 2 | **Definite noun** → use **the** |
| 3 | **Indefinite noun** → use **a/an** |
| 4 | **a/an** ❌ with plural/uncountable nouns — but **the** ✅ works with them |

---

## 3️⃣ Position of Article in a Sentence

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Article + Noun | **a** book |
| Article + Adjective + Noun | **a** red book |
| Article + Adverb + Adjective + Noun | **a** very red book |
| So/As/Too/This/That/How + Adj + **a** + Noun | I've never seen **this good a** player |
| Such/Quite + **a** + Adjective + Noun | **such a** brilliant idea |

⚠️ Article always sits at the **start of the noun phrase**.
⚠️ Adjective alone can't follow an article without a noun → "He is **a poor**." ❌ → "He is **a poor boy**." ✅

---

## 4️⃣ A vs AN — it's about SOUND, not spelling!

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Vowel **sound** → **an** | an hour, an honest girl, an FBI agent |
| Consonant **sound** → **a** | a university, a European, a one-eyed man |

### 🔥 Exam Favourites

| Take **A** | Take **AN** |
|---|---|
| a Unit, a Union, a Unique box, a Utopian scheme, a University, a Useful/Useless thing, a Ewe, a Eulogy, a European | an Orange, an Onion, an Owl, an Hour, an Heir/Heiress, an Honour, an Honest girl, an Honorary degree, an Honourable man |
| a Hotel, a SEBI employee, a NASA employee | an X-ray, an FIR, an SBI employee, an FBI agent, an NIA employee, an MEA delegation |

💡 **Trick**: "one-handed" → **a** (sounds like "w"); "Xerox" → **a** (sounds like "z"); "Unidentified" → **an** (starts with real vowel sound "uh").

---

## 5️⃣ Special Uses of Indefinite Article (a/an)

- As **ONE** → I ate **a** mango (NOT with hundred/dozen/thousand/million/lakh — those always take **a**: *a hundred*, *a dozen*)
- In place of **"per"** → 60 km **an** hour, rupees **a** kilo
- Before **half/quarter** in fractions → **a** quarter-hour
- Describing **profession/job/title** → She works as **a** manager
- With **verbs used as nouns** → He had **a** look, **a** walk
- With singular countable noun in **exclamations** → What **a** day!

---

## 6️⃣ A/An vs ONE

| Point | a/an | one |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | what/which/who | how many |
| First mention | I saw **a** dog | I saw **one** dog *(=only 1)* |
| Counting | ❌ | ✅ |
| With only/just/exactly/over | uncommon | ✅ |
| Identity | He is **a** doctor ✅ | He is one doctor ❌ |
| General truth | **A** tiger is dangerous ✅ | One tiger is dangerous ❌ |
| Exclamation | What **a** day! ✅ | What one day! ❌ |
| Before "one" | **a** one-rupee coin ✅ | one one-rupee coin ❌ |

---

## 7️⃣ Definite Article "THE" — When a Noun Becomes Definite

```
Noun becomes DEFINITE when:
   ├── it is REPEATED
   ├── followed by a CLAUSE / PHRASE (of+noun, prep+noun)
   ├── followed by an ADVERB or PARTICIPLE
   └── preceded by ORDINAL number, SUPERLATIVE degree,
       or specifying words (only, same, whole, very)
```
⚠️ If the phrase/clause still means **"one of many"** → stays **indefinite** (use a/an).

### Number Diagram
```
          NUMBERS
             │
   ┌─────────┴─────────┐
 ORDINAL              CARDINAL
 (sequence)           (count)
 first, second...     one, two, three...

 THE + ordinal + noun   = the second page
 noun + cardinal        = page two
 cardinal + noun        = two boys
```

**Examples:** the file **which was given** to you | the villagers **who approached** the officer | **the** only mistake | **the** same colour

---

## 8️⃣ "THE" with Degrees of Adjectives

| Degree | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | **The + Adj** = Adjective as noun (general sense) | the poor = poor people |
| Comparative | **the** used when comparative acts as subject/object, before "of", or shows relative change | Reena is **the** cleverer of the two |
| Superlative | **the** used, EXCEPT (a) "most" = "very", (b) noun compared with itself in different situations | Radhika is best at her work *(no "the" — compared with herself)* |

---

## 9️⃣ Adjective Acting as Noun (the + adjective = a group of people)

**the poor, the rich, the needy, the elderly, the weak, the powerful, the blind, the injured, the youth**

🚫 Never add "s": ~~the poors~~ / ~~the riches~~ — already plural in meaning!

### Nationality Trap
```
English / French  →  Noun = language ("she speaks French" – no article)
                  →  Adjective = nationality → "the English", "the French" (people)

Hindu / Indian    →  already proper nouns → "the Hindus", "the Indians" (add -s)
```

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Fixed "Go to ___" Phrases (always with THE)

Go to **the** bank / **the** post office / **the** police station / **the** cinema / **the** theatre / **the** opera
Go to **the** doctor / **the** dentist / **the** lawyer
Go to **the** forest / **the** beach / **the** seaside / **the** town

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Article with Proper Noun (used as a Common Noun)

```
A + Proper Noun   (WITHOUT "of")  → "she is a Shreya Ghoshal" (someone LIKE her)
THE + Adj + Proper Noun (WITH "of") → "the Virat Kohli of our office" (BEST of the category)
```
📌 Plain name with no article: "I am looking for **Shreya**." (just a person's name)

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Article in Subject–Verb Agreement (SVA)

| Situation | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Two nouns = SAME person/thing | Article only before 1st noun + **singular verb** | The actor and director **was** present |
| Two nouns = DIFFERENT persons/things | Article before EACH noun + **plural verb** | The Captain and **the** Coach **were** present |

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Article in Appositive Phrases (Name, ___, ...)

| Type | Article | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Unique title (only one such person) | **the** | Gandhiji, **the** father of the nation |
| One of many (non-unique) | **a** | My friend, **a** computer engineer |

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Other Special Cases

- Measuring expressions with **"by"** → by **the** kilo, by **the** dozen
- **the** radio, **the** cinema, **the** theatre — but **television/TV** = no article
- Drop "**the**" from radio/cinema/theatre when talking about them as an **art form/profession** → "He's worked in radio all his life."

---

## ⚡ Common SSC Traps (memorise before exam)

1. Hundred/Thousand/Dozen/Million/Lakh → always **a** hundred, **a** thousand (not "the", not bare)
2. Rate words → times **a** day, km **an** hour, rupees **a** kilo
3. Adjective needs a noun after it: "a poor **boy**", not "a poor."
4. So/As/Too/How/This/That + Adj + **a** + Noun (article AFTER adjective)
5. Comparative needs "the" only with "of" / as subject-object / relative increase pattern
6. "The English/French" = people; "the Hindus/Indians" = already plural noun
7. Never write "the poors" / "the riches"
8. Acronym read as a word (NASA, SEBI) → no "the"; abbreviation read letter-by-letter (the RBI, the FBI, the WHO) → needs "the"
9. Planets (Mars, Jupiter) → no article; but the Sun/Earth/Moon → need "the"
10. TV/television → no article; radio/cinema/theatre (as institution) → "the"
