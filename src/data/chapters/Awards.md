## India's Civilian Honours

### Bharat Ratna
India's **highest civilian honour** — awarded for exceptional service in any field.

**Recent recipients:**
- **Karpoori Thakur** (2024, posthumous) — Former Bihar CM, social reformer
- **LK Advani** (2024) — Former Deputy PM, BJP co-founder
- **PV Narasimha Rao** (2024, posthumous) — Former PM, architect of liberalisation
- **Chaudhary Charan Singh** (2024, posthumous) — Former PM, farmers' champion
- **MS Swaminathan** (2024, posthumous) — Father of Green Revolution

> **Note:** 2024 had a record 5 Bharat Ratna awardees announced simultaneously.

## Quick Facts

- 🏆 **Nobel Prize** established by **Alfred Nobel** (1895); first awarded **1901**
- 🎬 **Oscars** — awarded by **Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences (AMPAS)**
- 📚 **Booker Prize** — annual literary prize for English-language fiction
- 🕊️ **Nobel Peace Prize** — only Nobel decided in **Oslo, Norway** (others in Stockholm, Sweden)
- 🇮🇳 **First Bharat Ratna:** C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, C.V. Raman (all 1954)
