# Government Schemes — Current Affairs Revision

## Financial Inclusion Schemes

### PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
- **Launched:** August 28, 2014
- **Goal:** Universal banking access for all households
- **Features:** Zero-balance account, RuPay debit card, ₹1 lakh accident insurance, ₹30,000 life cover
- **Status:** 500+ crore accounts opened (as of 2024)

### PM MUDRA Yojana
Three categories of loans for non-corporate, non-farm small enterprises:

| Category | Loan Amount |
|----------|------------|
| **Shishu** | Up to ₹50,000 |
| **Kishore** | ₹50,001 – ₹5 lakh |
| **Tarun** | ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh |

> **Exam Tip:** MUDRA = Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency

## Housing Schemes

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)

**PMAY-Urban** (Housing for All by 2022 — extended)
- Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)
- Subsidy rates by income category:

| Category | Income | Subsidy Rate | Max Loan |
|----------|--------|-------------|----------|
| EWS | Up to ₹3 lakh | 6.5% | ₹6 lakh |
| LIG | ₹3–6 lakh | 6.5% | ₹6 lakh |
| MIG-I | ₹6–12 lakh | 4% | ₹9 lakh |
| MIG-II | ₹12–18 lakh | 3% | ₹12 lakh |

**PMAY-Gramin** (Rural)
- Target: Pucca houses for rural homeless
- Assistance: ₹1.20 lakh (plains), ₹1.30 lakh (hilly/NE)

## Agricultural Schemes

### PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Income support:** ₹6,000/year in 3 equal instalments (₹2,000 each)
- **Eligibility:** Small and marginal farmers (up to 2 hectares)
- **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** to bank accounts

### PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Launched:** 2016
- Crop insurance for farmers
- **Premium:** 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, 5% for commercial crops

### PM Kusum Scheme
Full name: **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Uttham Mahabhiyan**
- Solar pumps for farmers
- Grid-connected solar plants on barren land

## Health Schemes

### Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
- **Coverage:** ₹5 lakh per family per year
- **Target:** 10 crore+ poor and vulnerable families
- **Secondary and tertiary** care hospitalization
- Implemented by **National Health Authority (NHA)**

> **Exam Tip:** World's largest government-funded health insurance scheme by number of beneficiaries.

### Mission Indradhanush
- Universal Immunization Programme intensification
- Aims to vaccinate children against **12 vaccine-preventable diseases**

## Women Empowerment

### Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)
- **Launched:** January 22, 2015, Panipat (Haryana)
- Addresses declining Child Sex Ratio (CSR)
- Joint initiative: Ministry of Women & Child Development + Health + Education

### PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
- Maternity benefit scheme: ₹5,000 for first live birth
- Direct cash transfer to pregnant/lactating women

### Stand-Up India
- Loans between ₹10 lakh – ₹1 crore
- At least one **SC/ST borrower** and one **woman borrower** per bank branch

## Skill Development

### PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)
- **Implementing body:** National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)
- Short-term skill training + Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

### PM Vishwakarma Yojana (2023)
- For 18 traditional crafts/trades (blacksmith, carpenter, potter, etc.)
- **Collateral-free credit:** ₹1 lakh (1st tranche) + ₹2 lakh (2nd tranche) at 5% interest
- Digital payment incentive, skill training

## Digital & Technology

### Digital India
- **Launched:** July 1, 2015
- **Pillar programmes:** BharatNet, Common Service Centres, DigiLocker, UMANG, e-Kranti

### PM Gati Shakti
- **Launched:** October 13, 2021
- Multimodal connectivity master plan
- **16 ministries** integrated on one digital platform
- Coordinated by **DPIIT** (Dept of Promotion of Industry & Internal Trade)

### UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
- Developed by **NPCI** (National Payments Corporation of India)
- Built on **IMPS** infrastructure
- Crossed **20 billion monthly transactions** in 2026

## Social Security

### Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
- **For:** Unorganised sector workers aged **18–40**
- **Pension:** ₹1,000 – ₹5,000/month after age 60
- **Regulated by:** PFRDA

### e-SHRAM Portal
- National database of unorganised workers
- Linked to **Aadhaar**
- Ministry of **Labour & Employment**

## Environment

### National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC)
- **Launched:** 2008
- **8 National Missions:**
  1. National Solar Mission
  2. Enhanced Energy Efficiency
  3. Sustainable Habitat
  4. Water Mission
  5. Himalayan Ecosystem
  6. Green India
  7. Sustainable Agriculture
  8. Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change

### Green Credit Programme
- Under **Environment Protection Act 1986**
- Ministry of **Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)**
- Voluntary environmental actions get tradeable credits
