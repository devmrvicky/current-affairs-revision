# International Organizations — Current Affairs Revision

## United Nations System

### UN Overview
- **Founded:** October 24, 1945
- **Headquarters:** New York City, USA
- **Members:** 193 member states
- **Official languages:** Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish

### Principal Organs

| Organ | Function | HQ |
|-------|----------|-----|
| General Assembly | Deliberative body — all 193 members | New York |
| Security Council | Peace & security — 5 permanent + 10 elected | New York |
| International Court of Justice | Judicial organ | The Hague, Netherlands |
| Secretariat | Administrative body | New York |
| Economic & Social Council | Economic coordination | New York |
| Trusteeship Council | Suspended since 1994 | New York |

### UN Security Council (UNSC)
**P5 (Permanent Members with Veto Power):**
- 🇺🇸 United States
- 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
- 🇫🇷 France
- 🇨🇳 China
- 🇷🇺 Russia

> **India's UNSC bid:** India seeks permanent membership. Has served on UNSC 8 times as non-permanent member.

## Financial Institutions

### IMF (International Monetary Fund)
- **Established:** 1944 (Bretton Woods Conference)
- **HQ:** Washington D.C., USA
- **Role:** Lender of last resort for balance of payment crises
- **India's current account at IMF:** Special Drawing Rights (SDR)
- **Managing Director 2024:** Kristalina Georgieva (Bulgaria)
- Publishes **World Economic Outlook (WEO)** twice a year

### World Bank Group
- **Established:** 1944
- **HQ:** Washington D.C., USA
- **President 2024:** Ajay Banga (India-American)
- **Five institutions:**
  1. IBRD (International Bank for Reconstruction and Development)
  2. IDA (International Development Association)
  3. IFC (International Finance Corporation)
  4. MIGA (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency)
  5. ICSID (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes)

---

## Trade Organizations

### WTO (World Trade Organization)
- **Established:** January 1, 1995 (replaced GATT)
- **HQ:** Geneva, Switzerland
- **DG 2024:** Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala (Nigeria) — first woman, first African DG
- **Members:** 166 countries

### OECD (Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development)
- **HQ:** Paris, France
- **Members:** 38 (mostly developed nations)
- India is not a member but a "key partner"

---

## Regional Organizations

### G7 / G8
- **G7 Members:** USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Canada + EU
- Russia suspended since 2014 (Crimea annexation)
- 2024 G7 Summit: Fasano, Italy

### G20
- **Members:** 19 countries + EU + African Union (AU joined 2023)
- 2023 G20 Presidency: **India** (New Delhi Summit)
- 2024 G20 Presidency: **Brazil**
- 2025 G20 Presidency: **South Africa**

### BRICS
- Original: Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa
- **2024 expansion:** Iran, Egypt, Ethiopia, UAE, Saudi Arabia
- **2026:** Indonesia joined
- **Chairperson 2024:** Russia

### SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation)
- **HQ:** Beijing, China
- India joined as full member in **2017**
- Pakistan also joined in 2017
- Belarus joined **2024**

### ASEAN
- **10 members:** Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam
- **Secretariat:** Jakarta, Indonesia
- **Established:** 1967, Bangkok

---

## Security Organizations

### NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization)
- **Founded:** April 4, 1949 (Washington Treaty)
- **HQ:** Brussels, Belgium
- **Secretary-General 2024:** Mark Rutte (Netherlands)
- **Members:** 32 (Finland joined 2023, Sweden joined 2024)
- Collective defence: **Article 5** — attack on one = attack on all

### INTERPOL
- **Full name:** International Criminal Police Organization
- **HQ:** Lyon, France
- **Members:** 195 countries
- Issues Red Notices, Blue Notices, etc.

---

## Non-Proliferation & Arms

### FATF (Financial Action Task Force)
- **Established:** 1989 by **G7 Paris Summit**
- Combats money laundering & terrorist financing
- **Grey list / Black list** countries
- India became member in **2010**

### NSG (Nuclear Suppliers Group)
- Formed in **1974** after India's Pokhran-I nuclear test
- Controls export of nuclear materials
- India **not a member** (ongoing campaign to join)

### CTBT (Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty)
- Adopted: **1996** by UNGA
- **Not yet in force** — 8 Annex II states haven't ratified (USA, China, India, Pakistan, etc.)
