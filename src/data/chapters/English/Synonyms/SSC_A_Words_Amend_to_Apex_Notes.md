# SSC English Vocabulary Notes — A Words (Amend–Apex)

> **Format:** Tone → Meaning → SSC-level Context Sentence → 2 SSC/PYQ-oriented Synonyms → Antonyms  
> **Note:** Context sentences are newly written at an SSC-style/high-difficulty level; they are not verbatim SSC questions. Synonym/antonym selection has been cross-checked against SSC PYQ repositories and the supplied vocabulary page. Where a word has limited directly indexed SSC evidence, the pair is kept to the supplied book's SSC-oriented vocabulary set rather than adding random synonyms.

## 90. Amend

**Word:** Amend  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** To make a small correction or improvement; संशोधन करना, सुधारना।  

**Context Sentence:** The committee agreed to **amend** the regulation after discovering that its ambiguous wording could undermine the intended safeguard.

**Synonyms:** Alter, Rectify  
**Antonyms:** Worsen, Damage  

---

## 91. Amiable / Amicable

**Word:** Amiable / Amicable  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** Friendly, pleasant, and free from hostility; मिलनसार, मैत्रीपूर्ण।  

**Context Sentence:** Despite their sharply divergent views, the two negotiators maintained an **amiable/amicable** atmosphere throughout the prolonged discussion.

**Synonyms:** Friendly, Cordial  
**Antonyms:** Hostile, Unfriendly  

---

## 92. Amorphous

**Word:** Amorphous  
**Tone:** 0  
**Meaning:** Without a clearly defined shape, form, or structure; अनियमित आकार वाला, निराकार।  

**Context Sentence:** The proposal remained **amorphous**, lacking the precise objectives and institutional framework required for effective implementation.

**Synonyms:** Shapeless, Nebulous  
**Antonyms:** Definite, Well-defined  

---

## 93. Ample

**Word:** Ample  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** Enough or more than enough; plentiful; पर्याप्त, प्रचुर।  

**Context Sentence:** The investigation produced **ample** evidence to establish a consistent pattern, despite the witnesses' conflicting accounts.

**Synonyms:** Sufficient, Abundant  
**Antonyms:** Meagre, Scarce  

---

## 94. Amplify

**Word:** Amplify  
**Tone:** 0  
**Meaning:** To increase the strength, volume, intensity, or extent of something; बढ़ाना, तीव्र करना।  

**Context Sentence:** The report was carefully framed to **amplify** the significance of the statistical trend without distorting the underlying evidence.

**Synonyms:** Magnify, Boost  
**Antonyms:** Reduce, Lessen  

---

## 95. Amuse / Amusement / Amusing

**Word:** Amuse / Amusement / Amusing  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** To entertain or cause someone to find something funny or enjoyable; मनोरंजन करना, हँसाना।  

**Context Sentence:** The seemingly **amusing** anecdote was deliberately inserted to relieve the tension without weakening the seriousness of the argument.

**Synonyms:** Entertain, Delight  
**Antonyms:** Bore, Displease  

---

## 96. Analogous

**Word:** Analogous  
**Tone:** 0  
**Meaning:** Similar or comparable in certain respects; अनुरूप, समान।  

**Context Sentence:** The researchers argued that the two institutional crises were **analogous** in structure, although their historical causes were fundamentally different.

**Synonyms:** Similar, Comparable  
**Antonyms:** Dissimilar, Different

## 97. Ancestor

**Word:** Ancestor  
**Tone:** 0  
**Meaning:** A person, usually from an earlier generation, from whom one is descended; पूर्वज।  

**Context Sentence:** The inscription enabled historians to establish that the ruler was a direct **ancestor** of the dynasty's later monarchs.

**Synonyms:** Forefather, Predecessor  
**Antonyms:** Descendant, Successor

## 98. Ancient

**Word:** Ancient  
**Tone:** 0  
**Meaning:** Belonging to the very distant past; प्राचीन।  

**Context Sentence:** The archaeologists uncovered an **ancient** settlement whose administrative records offered rare evidence of early urban governance.

**Synonyms:** Antique, Age-old  
**Antonyms:** Modern, Recent  

---

## 99. Annihilate

**Word:** Annihilate  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** To destroy completely; नष्ट कर देना, समूल नष्ट करना।  

**Context Sentence:** The objective was not merely to defeat the opposing force but to **annihilate** its capacity to reorganise in the region.

**Synonyms:** Destroy, Obliterate  
**Antonyms:** Create, Preserve  

---

## 100. Annoy

**Word:** Annoy  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** To irritate or make someone slightly angry; चिढ़ाना, परेशान करना।  

**Context Sentence:** The repeated interruptions began to **annoy** even the most patient members of the panel, who had initially remained silent.

**Synonyms:** Irritate, Offend  
**Antonyms:** Satisfy, Please  

---

## 101. Anodyne

**Word:** Anodyne  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** Soothing or harmless; especially not likely to cause offence; दर्द-निवारक, शांतिदायक, अहानिकर।  

**Context Sentence:** The spokesperson adopted an **anodyne** tone, carefully avoiding any statement that could intensify the already sensitive dispute.

**Synonyms:** Benign, Inoffensive  
**Antonyms:** Harmful, Offensive  

---

## 102. Anomalous

**Word:** Anomalous  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** Deviating from what is standard, normal, or expected; असामान्य, बेमेल।  

**Context Sentence:** The unusually high reading was considered **anomalous** because it contradicted the pattern observed in every other trial.

**Synonyms:** Peculiar, Abnormal  
**Antonyms:** Customary, Normal  

---

## 103. Anticipate

**Word:** Anticipate  
**Tone:** 0  
**Meaning:** To regard as probable; to expect or predict; पूर्वानुमान करना, अपेक्षा करना।  

**Context Sentence:** The administration had **anticipated** that a sudden rise in demand would place considerable pressure on the existing infrastructure.

**Synonyms:** Expect, Predict  
**Antonyms:** Doubt, Disbelieve  

---

## 104. Antipathy

**Word:** Antipathy  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** A deep-seated feeling of dislike or aversion; गहरी घृणा, प्रबल अरुचि।  

**Context Sentence:** His long-standing **antipathy** towards corruption made him unusually uncompromising during the ethics inquiry.

**Synonyms:** Dislike, Aversion  
**Antonyms:** Affection, Affinity  

---

## 105. Antique

**Word:** Antique  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** Belonging to an ancient period; especially an old object valued for its age; प्राचीन, पुरातन वस्तु।  

**Context Sentence:** The curator authenticated the **antique** manuscript by comparing its script, paper quality, and provenance with archival records.

**Synonyms:** Ancient, Outdated  
**Antonyms:** Modern, New  

---

## 106. Anxiety

**Word:** Anxiety  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** A feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease about an uncertain outcome; चिंता, घबराहट।  

**Context Sentence:** Prolonged uncertainty over the recruitment schedule created considerable **anxiety** among candidates who had already completed the selection process.

**Synonyms:** Worry, Nervousness  
**Antonyms:** Certainty, Calmness  

---

## 107. Anxious

**Word:** Anxious  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** Feeling or showing worry, nervousness, or unease; चिंतित, व्याकुल।  

**Context Sentence:** The candidates grew **anxious** as the examination authority postponed the result without providing a definite date.

**Synonyms:** Nervous, Apprehensive  
**Antonyms:** Calm, Unconcerned  

---

## 108. Apathy

**Word:** Apathy  
**Tone:** -  
**Meaning:** Lack of interest, enthusiasm, or concern; उदासीनता, अरुचि।  

**Context Sentence:** Public **apathy** towards repeated warnings allowed the preventable problem to persist until it became substantially more difficult to control.

**Synonyms:** Indifference, Passivity  
**Antonyms:** Enthusiasm, Interest  

---

## 109. Apex

**Word:** Apex  
**Tone:** +  
**Meaning:** The highest point or peak; शिखर, सर्वोच्च बिंदु।  

**Context Sentence:** After years of disciplined preparation, the scientist reached the **apex** of her career when her work received international recognition.

**Synonyms:** Peak, Summit  
**Antonyms:** Base, Nadir

## Research Validation — SSC PYQ Evidence Used

- **Amiable:** SSC GD 2019 asked **Friendly** as the synonym; SSC CPO 2019 asked **Hostile** as the antonym. Cordial is also directly tested with *Amicable* in SSC Stenographer 2019.
- **Amorphous:** SSC CHSL 2022 tested *Shapeless → Amorphous*; the supplied page gives **Nebulous** as the paired synonym.
- **Ample:** SSC GD 2021/2023 and SSC MTS 2021 tested **Sufficient**; SSC GD 2021 tested **Meagre** as the antonym. SSC CGL 2024 also tested the *Ample ↔ Abundant* relation.
- **Amplify:** SSC CPO 2019 tested **Magnify**; SSC MTS 2024 tested **Contract** as the antonym; SSC Stenographer 2024 tested **Lessen** as the antonym; SSC CHSL 2025 tested **Reduce**.
- **Annihilate:** SSC GD 2025 tested **Destroy** as the synonym.
- **Annoy:** SSC GD 2019 tested **Irritate**; SSC CPO 2022 tested **Satisfy** as the antonym.
- **Anodyne:** SSC CHSL 2020 tested **Benign** as the synonym.
- **Anomalous:** SSC CHSL 2019 tested **Peculiar**; SSC CHSL 2022 tested **Customary** as the antonym.
- **Anticipate:** SSC CHSL 2020 tested **Expect**; SSC MTS 2024 tested **Predict**.
- **Antipathy:** SSC GD 2021 tested the one-word meaning of strong/deep dislike; SSC CHSL 2025 tested **Antipathy** for “strong and deep hatred.”
- **Antique:** SSC MTS 2020/2024 and SSC CHSL 2024 tested **Ancient** as the synonym.
- **Anxiety:** SSC MTS 2020 tested **Worry**; SSC CGL 2020 tested **Certainty** as the antonym.
- **Anxious:** SSC MTS 2019 tested **Nervous**; SSC CHSL 2022 tested **Apprehensive**; SSC GD 2022 tested **Unconcerned** as the antonym.
- **Apathy:** SSC CHSL 2025 tested **Enthusiasm** as the antonym.
- **Apex:** SSC CHSL 2020 tested **Base** as the antonym; SSC-oriented vocabulary references pair it with **Peak/Summit/Zenith**.
