# Sports — Current Affairs Revision

## Olympics

### Paris 2024 Summary
India won **6 medals** at the Paris 2024 Olympics — the second-best tally ever.

| Medal | Athlete | Event |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Silver 🥈 | Neeraj Chopra | Javelin Throw |
| Bronze 🥉 | Manu Bhaker | 10m Air Pistol (W) |
| Bronze 🥉 | Manu Bhaker & Sarabjot Singh | 10m Air Pistol Mixed Team |
| Bronze 🥉 | Swapnil Kusale | 50m Rifle 3P |
| Bronze 🥉 | Indian Hockey Team | Men's Hockey |
| Bronze 🥉 | Aman Sehrawat | Wrestling (57kg Freestyle) |

> **Exam Tip:** Manu Bhaker became the first Indian to win 2 medals in a single Olympic Games since Independence.

## Major Awards in Sports

### Khel Ratna (India's Highest Sporting Award)
Formerly **Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna**, renamed to **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award** in August 2021.

### Arjuna Award
- Second highest sporting honour in India
- Instituted in **1961**
- Given for outstanding performance over 4 years

### Dronacharya Award
- For outstanding coaches
- Named after Guru Dronacharya (from Mahabharata)

### Dhyan Chand Award
- For lifetime contribution to sports
- Named after hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand

## Chess

### D. Gukesh — World Chess Champion 2024
- Beat **Ding Liren** (China) in Singapore
- Became the **youngest ever World Chess Champion** at age 18
- Third Indian to win the title (after Viswanathan Anand)

### Viswanathan Anand
- Won World Chess Championship **5 times** (2000, 2007, 2008, 2010, 2012)
- Received **Padma Vibhushan** (2007) and **Padma Bhushan** (2000)

## India-Specific

### Sports Authority of India (SAI)
- Established: **1984**
- Headquarters: **New Delhi**
- Runs schemes like **TOPS** (Target Olympic Podium Scheme)

### TOPS (Target Olympic Podium Scheme)
- Launched in **2014**
- Provides elite funding to top athletes for Olympic preparation
