## 0. 🧭 Master Flow (हर Question में यही 7 Steps)

| Step | क्या करें |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sentence type पहचानो: Assertive / Interrogative / Imperative / Exclamatory / Optative |
| 2 | **Reporting Verb (RV)** बदलो (said → told / asked / ordered / exclaimed…) |
| 3 | **Joiner** लगाओ: `that` / `if-whether` / वही WH-word / `to + V1` |
| 4 | **Pronoun** बदलो → SON rule |
| 5 | **Tense** बदलो (सिर्फ़ जब RV past हो) |
| 6 | **Adverb / Determiner** बदलो (सिर्फ़ जब RV past हो) |
| 7 | `" "`, `?`, `!` हटाओ; प्रश्न में सीधा order (S + V) |

### RV का Tense तय करता है क्या बदलेगा

| Reporting Verb | Pronoun | Tense | Adverb |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Past** (said, told, asked…) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Present / Future** (says, has announced, will remind) | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

## 1. Basics (PDF pp. 4–6)

**Formula:** `Reporting Verb + Joiner + Reported Speech`

| Direct Speech | Indirect Speech |
|---|---|
| She said, "I am tired." | She said that she was tired. |
| He asked, "Where is the park?" | He asked where the park was. |
| He said to me, "Have you watched this movie?" | He asked me if I had watched that movie. |
| Radhika said, "I will go shopping." | Radhika said that she would go shopping. |
| The teacher said to the students, "India has a rigid constitution." | The teacher told the students that India has a rigid constitution. *(fact → no change)* |

- Direct = exact words, inverted commas में।
- Indirect = अपने words में, quotes नहीं; pronoun-tense-adverb बदलते हैं।
- Conversion: *"I am going now"* → *She was going then.*

## 2. Pronoun Rule — **SON (1-2-3)** (PDF pp. 7–8)

| Person | बदलाव किसके अनुसार |
|---|---|
| **1st** (I, we, my, our) | **S**ubject of RV |
| **2nd** (you, your) | **O**bject of RV |
| **3rd** (he, she, they, it) | **N**o change |

- Radhika said to me, "**I** will tell **you** about **her**." → Radhika told me that **she** would tell **me** about **her**.
- Mohit said to Shivani, "**You** should not insult **me** in front of **her**." → Mohit told Shivani that **she** should not insult **him** in front of **her**.
- Abhishek said to Aakash, "**You** played very well yesterday." → Abhishek told Aakash that **he** had played very well the previous day.

### 'We' के 4 केस

| 'We' कौन? | Indirect में | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker + **दूसरे** (सुनने वाला शामिल नहीं) | **they** | Ravi said to you, "We should help you." → Ravi told you that **they** should help you. |
| Speaker + **listener** | **you** | Ravi said to you, "We should help this man." → Ravi told you that **you** should help that man. |
| **सब लोग** (general / universal) | **we** | The teacher said, "We are mortal." → The teacher said that **we** are mortal. |
| Speaker + **reporter (me)** | **we** | Radhika said to me, "We should complete it." → Radhika told me that **we** should complete it. |

- The RWA members said, "**We** want to see the audited accounts before **we** make more payments." → …said that **they** wanted to see the audited accounts before **they** made more payments. *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- The professor said to the media persons, "From **our** research **we** have come to the conclusion…" → …that from **their** research **they** had come to the conclusion… *(CGL Pre 2020)*

## 3. Tense Rule (RV past हो तब) (PDF pp. 9–10)

💡 **एक कदम पीछे:** Present → Past · Past → Past Perfect

| Direct | Indirect | Example |
|---|---|---|
| do / does / **V1** | did / **V2** | "I know" → he knew |
| is / am / are | was / were | "I am happy" → she was happy |
| has / have | had | "It has been a week" → it had been a week |
| did / **V2** | had + **V3** | "I went to Ayodhya" → he had gone to Ayodhya |
| was / were | had been | "You were warned" → he had been warned |
| shall / will → **would** (should) · can → **could** · may → **might** | would / should / could / might | "I shall need" → he would need · "I can swim" → he could swim |
| **Other modals** (must, should, would, could, might, ought to) | **No change** | "silence must be maintained" → must ही रहेगा |
| **Past perfect / Past perfect continuous** | **No change** | "I had paid" → had paid |

- Ram said to his wife, "I went to Ayodhya." → Ram told his wife that he **had gone** to Ayodhya.
- Radhika said to me, "I am happy." → Radhika told me that she **was** happy.
- Continuous / perfect भी इसी पंक्ति से: is leaving → **was** leaving · has come → **had** come.

## 4. Adverb / Determiner Change (RV past हो तब) (PDF p. 11)

| Direct | Indirect |
|---|---|
| this / these | that / those |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| ago | before |
| yet | till then |
| today | that day |
| tonight | that night |
| yesterday | the previous day |
| tomorrow | the following day / the next day |
| this morning / this evening | that morning / that evening |
| next week | the following week / the week after |
| last week | the previous week / the week before |

**PYQ में आए:** last night → the previous night · next year → the following year · this year → that year · this whole week → that whole week · tomorrow morning → the next morning · right now → at once

**➕ अतिरिक्त:** thus → so · come → go · last year → the previous year · next month → the following month

⚠️ SSC-2025 key में *"Do you not realize how serious this is?"* → *…how serious **this was**.* (यहाँ this नहीं बदला)

## 6. 5 Types — RV + Joiner एक नज़र में (PDF pp. 14–15)

| Type | Reporting Verb | Joiner | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assertive** (Statement) | said (object नहीं हो तो) · said to → told, informed, replied, answered | **that** | He said, "I am tired." → He said **that** he was tired. |
| **Interrogative** (Question) | asked, inquired, wanted to know, demanded | Yes/No → **if / whether** · WH → **वही WH-word** | She said, "Where do you live?" → She asked **where** I lived. |
| **Imperative** (Order, Request, Advice) | said to → ordered, requested, advised, warned, begged, urged, forbade, suggested | **to + V1** / **not to + V1** | He said, "Open the door." → He told me **to open** the door. |
| **Exclamatory** (Feelings) | exclaimed with + joy / sorrow / surprise / disgust… ; cried out, shouted, called out | **that** (assertive बनाओ) | He said, "Alas! I failed." → He exclaimed with sorrow **that** he had failed. |
| **Optative** (Wish, Prayer, Blessing, Curse) | wished, prayed, blessed, cursed, hoped, bade | **that** + might/could | He said, "May God bless you." → He prayed **that** God might bless me. |

## 7. Assertive (PDF pp. 16–17, 38)

**Rule:** `, " "` → **that** · said → said · said to → **told**

**RV भाव के अनुसार:**

| भाव | RV | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Verification | confirm | confirmed that… |
| Official news / facts | report | reported that… |
| Public declaration | announce | announced that… |
| Clarification / reason | explain | explained that… / explained how… |
| Visuals / details | describe | described how… / described [object] |
| Dissatisfaction | complain | complained that… / complained about… |
| Regret / remorse | apologize | apologized for + V-ing |
| Desperation / begging | plead | pleaded with [person] to… |
| Deep wish / spiritual | pray | prayed that… |
| Idea / recommendation | suggest | suggested V-ing / suggested that… |
| Formal plan | propose | proposed V-ing / proposed that… |
| General | say | said that… |
| Written | write | wrote that… |

**Examples**
- Kavya says, "I do not like it anymore." → Kavya says that she does not like it anymore. *(present RV → no tense change)*
- Ram said to his wife, "I went to Ayodhya." → Ram told his wife that he had gone to Ayodhya.
- He said, "I shall need a copy of the documents." → He said that he would need a copy of the documents. *(CGL Mains 2024)*

## 8. Interrogative (PDF pp. 15, 26–34)

| Rule | विवरण |
|---|---|
| RV | asked / inquired (enquired) / wanted to know / demanded |
| **Yes/No** Q (is, are, do, does, did, have, can, will… से शुरू) | **if / whether** — दोनों साथ ❌ (whether if ✗) |
| **WH** Q (what, why, where, when, how, whom, who) | **वही WH-word** |
| Order | WH / if + **Subject + Verb** (सीधा order) |
| हटाओ | do / does / did · `?` |
| **that** | ❌ कभी नहीं (asked that ✗) |
| Negative Q | **not** बना रहेगा |
| Who / What = subject | order वही, सिर्फ़ tense: *who broke → who had broken* |
| नाम / vocative (Meena, Dear daughter) | हटाओ या adverb बनाओ (affectionately) |

✍️ Sir का नोट: *Who is Radhika?* → *who Radhika was* (order बदलता है)

**Examples**
- He said to me, "Have you watched this movie?" → He asked me **if** I had watched that movie.
- He said, "Are you coming?" → He asked **if** I was coming.
- She said, "Where do you live?" → She asked **where** I lived.
- The teacher said to the students, "Who broke the window pane?" → The teacher asked the students **who had broken** the window pane. ❌ *asked that who… · whom had broken*
- "Whom did you see at the shopping mall today?" I asked my daughter. → I asked my daughter **whom she had seen** at the shopping mall **that day**. *(CGL Pre 2020)*
- The man said to his wife, "What have I done to you that you are treating me so cruelly?" → The man asked his wife **what he had done** to her that she was treating him so cruelly. *(SSC 2025)*
- "Why is everybody leaving so early today?" said the manager. → The manager asked **why everybody was leaving** so early **that day**. *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- He said to the mechanic, "Will you have the car ready by tomorrow morning?" → He asked the mechanic **if he would have** the car ready by **the next morning**. *(CHSL Mains 2023)*
- He said to him, "Is your name not Ahmed?" → He enquired **whether** his name was **not** Ahmed. *(CGL Pre 2022)*
- The man said, "Do you not realize how serious this is?" → The man asked **if** I did **not** realize how serious this was. *(SSC 2025 key)*
- "Meena, will you assist me to pack our belongings?" asked her mother. → Meena's mother asked her **whether** she would assist her to pack their belongings. ❌ *whether if* *(CGL Pre 2022)*
- "Who is it?" Vikram asked, his euphoria fading. → **With his euphoria fading**, Vikram asked **who it was**. *(CHSL Pre 2022)*
- "Dear daughter," she said caressing her cheeks, "have you come to comfort me in my time of need?" → Caressing her daughter's cheeks, she asked **affectionately if she had come** to comfort her in her time of need. *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- Aditya said to Kavya, "What do you prefer to do in your free time — painting or basketball practice?" → Aditya asked Kavya **what she preferred** to do in her free time — painting or basketball practice. *(CPO Mains 2024)*

## 9. Imperative (PDF pp. 35–47)

**Formula:** `V1 …` / `Never, Don't + V1` → **to + V1** / **not to + V1**

| Direct clue | भाव | Reporting Verb |
|---|---|---|
| Please…, Kindly… | Request / Pleading | requested, begged, pleaded, implored, entreated |
| Captain / Boss / Judge का सीधा आदेश | Command / Order | ordered, commanded |
| बराबर वाले / सामान्य ("Open the door") | General instruction | asked, told, directed, instructed |
| Always…, You should… (बड़ा / doctor / expert) | Advice / Guidance | advised, counseled, urged |
| Don't…, Never… | Prohibition | forbade, prohibited, ordered not to, warned not to |
| Watch out!, Be careful!, Beware | Warning / Caution | warned, cautioned |
| Let's…, Let us… | Proposal / Suggestion | proposed, suggested |
| Have some tea, Come to my party | Invitation / Offer | invited, offered |
| Shoot!, Attack! (military / urgency) | Strong command | commanded, ordered, shouted at |
| Go on… *(PYQ)* | Encouragement | encouraged |
| Don't forget to… *(PYQ)* | Reminder | reminded … to |

**Special Rules**
1. **forbade / prohibited** के साथ अलग से **not नहीं** लगता (पहले से negative हैं)।
2. **Let's / Let us** → suggested / proposed **+ that + subject + should** — ❌ *to + V1 नहीं* (Sir ने *suggested to + V1* काटा है)।
3. **Let + 3rd person** → *was/were to*.
4. **Passive imperative** (*ordered the fellow to be arrested*) ↔ Direct: **"Arrest the fellow."**
5. **Imperative + or / otherwise** → *ordered … to V1 or they would be punished.*
6. एक से ज़्यादा RV (*encouraged and advised*) ↔ Direct में सिर्फ़ **said**।

**Examples**
- He said, "Open the door." → He told me **to open** the door.
- He said, "Don't shout." → He told me **not to shout**.
- He said to his brother, "Please stop playing loud music." → He **requested** his brother **to stop** playing loud music. *(SSC 2025)*
- She said, "Do it right now." → She told me **to do it at once**. *(SSC 2025)*
- He said to his friend, "Let us leave for the trek tomorrow." → He **suggested** to his friend **that they should leave** for the trek **the next day**. *(CPO Mains 2023)*
- "Let's not have non-vegetarian meals in summer," Aruna said to her son. → Aruna **suggested** to her son **that they should not have** non-vegetarian meals in summer. *(CHSL Pre 2020)*
- The librarian said, "Let no student be issued a book till next week." → The librarian said that no student **was to be issued** a book till **the following week**. *(CGL Mains 2020)*
- "Go on, apply for the job," said my best friend. → My best friend **encouraged** me **to apply** for the job. *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- "Don't forget to bring your umbrella," he said to her. → He **reminded** her **to bring** her umbrella. *(SSC 2025)*
- The captain shouted to the soldiers, "Advance immediately, or you will be punished!" → The captain **ordered** the soldiers **to advance** immediately **or they would be punished**. *(SSC 2025)*

## 10. Exclamatory (PDF pp. 48–59, 62–69)

**Rules** (Sir के हैंडराइटन नोट्स)
1. **said → exclaimed with** joy / sorrow / surprise / disgust… (या cried out, shouted, called out)
2. **Interjection** (Alas, Oh, Wow…) और `!` **हटाओ**
3. Exclamatory → **Assertive** बनाओ, **that** लगाओ
4. Tense / pronoun पहले की तरह बदलो
5. **How / What** → `that + S + was + very + adj` · **What a/an + noun** → `that it was a/an + noun`
6. **Where…!** → **nowhere**

**Examples**
- The man said, "Alas! I have missed the opportunity!" → The man **exclaimed with sorrow that** he had missed the opportunity.
- Monu said, "How simple she is!" → Monu **exclaimed with surprise that** she was **very simple**.
- Pawan said, "Where can this happiness be found!" → Pawan exclaimed with joy that that happiness could be found **nowhere**.
- They said, "Ah! Our bike has been stolen." → They exclaimed **sorrowfully** that their bike had been stolen.
- She said, "Wow, the police have arrested the burglar!" → She exclaimed **with joy / delight** that the police had arrested the burglar.
- The captain said, "What! He failed to kick the ball through the goalposts!" → The captain exclaimed **with disgust** that he had failed to kick the ball through the goalposts.
- "What an enormous pumpkin!" said the boy. → The boy exclaimed **with surprise that it was** an enormous pumpkin. *(Phase IX 2022)*
- Rakesh said, "Wow! What a beautiful house". → Rakesh exclaimed **that it was** a beautiful house. *(CHSL Mains 2023)*
- The Headmistress said, "What a performance!" → The Headmistress exclaimed that it was **a great** performance. *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- When Rohit saw Saurabh, he said, "Oh, how tall you have grown!" → …he exclaimed that he (Saurabh) **had grown very tall**. *(CGL Tier II 2019)*
- They cried, "Bravo! Well done." → They **shouted in joy that** they had done well. *(CGL Pre 2022)*
- "Hurrah! I have topped again," he said. "Congratulations," said I. → He exclaimed with joy that he had topped again. I congratulated him. *(Selection Post 2024)*
- He said, "Oh, how I would have loved to visit Kashmir this year." → He exclaimed that he **would have really loved** to visit Kashmir **that year**. † *(CGL Mains 2021)*

## 11. Optative (PDF pp. 50, 54, 58)

**Direct:** `May / Wish / Would that + S + V …!`
**Indirect:** `S + RV + that + S + might / could + V` (past form)

| भाव | RV |
|---|---|
| Wish / Desire | wished |
| Prayer | prayed |
| Blessing | wished / blessed |
| Curse | cursed |
| (और भी) | hoped, bade |

**Examples**
- The saint said to me, "May God bless you!" → The saint **blessed** me **that God might bless** me.
- Radhika said, "May he fail!" → Radhika **cursed** him **that he might fail**.
- They said to him, "Long live the king!" → They **prayed that the King might live long**.
- The old man said to her, "Good luck to you! May you succeed in your venture!" → The old man **wished her good luck and prayed that she might succeed** in her venture. *(CGL Mains 2020)*
- She said, "If only I hadn't trusted him!" → She said that she **wished** she hadn't trusted him. *(SSC 2025)*

## 12. Mixed Sentences (एक वाक्य में दो type) (PYQ)

**Rule:** हर हिस्से को उसके type के अनुसार बदलो, फिर *and / further / as* से जोड़ो।

- Reena said, "What a lovely scene! I wish I could stay here forever!" → Reena **exclaimed that it was a lovely scene**. She **further wished that she could stay there forever**. *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- "Let's go out for dinner. I've been home this whole week," said Anna. → Anna **suggested (that) we go out for dinner as she had been home that whole week**. *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- My grandfather said to me, "May God give you success in your new venture! My prayers will always be with you." ↔ My grandfather **wished that God should give me success** in my new venture. He further said that **his prayers would always be with me**. *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- He said to his soldiers, "Take this bird away. Its gilded cage reminds me of my father whom I imprisoned." ↔ He **ordered** his soldiers **to take that bird away**. Its gilded cage **reminded him** of his father whom **he had imprisoned**. *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- "Hurrah! I have topped again," he said. "Congratulations," said I. → He exclaimed with joy that he had topped again. I congratulated him.

## 13. Time Clauses of Past (PDF pp. 70–74)

**Direct:** `RV, "Past Simple + when / while / till + Past Simple"`

| क्या बदला | Example | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **कोई clause नहीं** | He said that while he **was cooking**, he **burnt** himself. | ⭐ **सबसे अच्छा** — time relation conjunction (while) से ही clear |
| **सिर्फ़ Main clause** | He said that he **had burnt** himself while he **was cooking**. | ✅ Acceptable / formal |
| **दोनों clause** | He said that while he **had been cooking**, he **had burnt** himself. | ⚠️ Acceptable पर discouraged — भारी और repetitive |
| **सिर्फ़ Time clause** | Priya kept … till her mother **had** exploded | ❌ गलत |

- Abhishek said to Akash, "You were cooking when I saw you first." → Abhishek told Akash that he **was cooking** when he **saw** him first.
- Pawan said, "I got angry when she cheated on him." → Pawan said that he **got** angry when she **cheated** on him.
- You said, "Priya kept watching television till her mother exploded at her." → You said that Priya **kept** watching television till her mother **exploded** at her. ⭐ *(CPO Mains 2023 — option (a); (b) had kept… = II; (d) दोनों = III)*
- She said, "Ruhi slipped when she was trying to board the bus." → She said that Ruhi **slipped** when she **was trying** to board the bus. ⭐ *(CGL Mains 2022; (a) had slipped = acceptable)*

## 14. Advanced — Conditional / Inversion (SSC 2025 Pattern) (PDF pp. 75–79, 129–141)

**Rules**
1. जो forms पहले से past हैं — **would / could / might / should, would have + V3, had + V3, were** — उन पर आगे **backshift नहीं** → Direct में वही रहेंगे।
2. **Inverted conditional** (**Had / Were / Should** + S …) → Direct में **वही inversion** रखो।
3. *Were + S + V3* का **If + S + were + V3** रूप भी सही है।
4. **Negative-adverb inversion** (Little did…) → tense वही रहता है।
5. **would → shall** (I के साथ)।
6. **If only → wished**।
7. **Unless + were …** → SSC key: Direct में **are** (और Sir ने *were* वाला option भी ✔ किया)।

| Indirect | Direct |
|---|---|
| The zoologist cautioned that the island's carrying capacity **might be exceeded should** invasive species proliferate unchecked. | The zoologist said, "The island's carrying capacity **might be exceeded should** invasive species proliferate unchecked." |
| The analyst observed that, **had** market liquidity vanished entirely, the exchange **would have suspended** trading. | The analyst said, "**Had** market liquidity vanished entirely, the exchange **would have suspended** trading." |
| Akash speculated that, **had** stochastic volatility been incorporated, the valuation **would perhaps have been** more traditional. | Akash speculated, "**Had** stochastic volatility **been** incorporated, the valuation **would perhaps have been** more traditional." |
| The music creator explained that, **were** the leitmotif transposed an octave higher, the tension **might dissipate** prematurely. | The music creator said, "**Were** the leitmotif transposed an octave higher, the tension **might dissipate** prematurely." |
| The analyst said that **were** the frameworks revised earlier, the crisis **could have been avoided**. | "**If** the frameworks **were** revised earlier, the crisis **could have been avoided**," said the analyst. |
| The archivist declared that, **unless** the humidity controls **were** recalibrated, the papyri **could** deteriorate beyond salvage. | The archivist declared, "Unless the humidity controls **are** recalibrated, the papyri **could** deteriorate beyond salvage." |
| The primatologist pointed out that grooming behaviour **would diminish if** the troop hierarchy **were** destabilised. | The primatologist said, "Grooming behaviour **would diminish if** the troop hierarchy **were** destabilised." *(वही)* |
| He said that **little did he realize** what repercussions his words **would have**. | "**Little did I realize** what repercussions my words **would have**," he said. |
| She said that she **would arrive** before noon. | She said, "I **shall arrive** before noon." |

## 15. Indirect → Direct (उल्टा नियम)

| Indirect | Direct |
|---|---|
| told, informed (told me) | said to |
| asked / inquired + if / whether | said to + **प्रश्न-order वापस** (Can I…?) |
| ordered, requested, advised (+ to V1) | said / said to + **V1** (Please…, Don't…) |
| exclaimed with… | interjection / What / How + `!` |
| wished, prayed, blessed | May …! |
| that · then · that day | " " · now · today |
| the previous day · the next day | yesterday · tomorrow |
| was / were · V2 · had + V3 | is / are · V1 (या V2) · V2 (या वही had + V3, अगर original past perfect था) |
| would · could · might | will / shall · can · may |
| he / she / they | I / you / we (context से) |
| **Universal truth / must / would have** | **वही रहते हैं** |

- Manya said that she didn't want any breakfast. → Manya said, "**I don't** want any breakfast." *(CPO Mains 2024)*
- He told her that he could not marry her then but he would surely do so the following year. → He said to her, "I **cannot** marry you **now** but I **will** surely do so **next year**." *(CPO Mains 2023)*
- Bibiya asked the doctor if she could get the discharge in the morning. → Bibiya asked the doctor, "**Can I** get the discharge in the morning?" *(Phase XI 2023)*
- The traveller asked the owner if he could stay in that tavern. The peasant replied that he could. → The traveller said, "**Can I** stay in **this** tavern?" The peasant replied, "**You can**." *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- Balaji said that he had already paid the installment when the notice came to him. → Balaji said, "**I had** already paid the installment when the notice **came to me**." *(CHSL 2020)*
- Rohan smiled and said that he thought Jojo liked him. → Rohan smiled and said, "**I think** Jojo **likes** me!" *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- Mr. Lucas told his wife not to wait for him as he would be late at the studio that night. → Mr. Lucas said to his wife, "**Don't** wait for **me** as **I will** be late at the studio **tonight**." *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- The official predicted that there would be heavy fog in Delhi the next day. → The official said, "**There will be** heavy fog in Delhi **tomorrow**." *(CPO Mains 2024)*
- Looking at his watch Barin Babu observed that they were running more or less on time. → …said, "**We are** running more or less on time." *(CPO Mains 2023)*
- My teacher forbids to trust one's relatives. → My teacher **says, "Never trust your relatives."** *(CGL Pre 2022)*
- Her mother advised her not to go too fast while reciting her poem. → Her mother said, "**Don't go** too fast while reciting **your** poem." *(CGL Tier II 2019)*
- Mother warned me not to make a mess of the living room again. → Mother warned, "**Don't make** a mess of the living room again!" *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- Mrs. Lisa suggested that we should purchase the property. → Mrs. Lisa said, "**Let us** purchase the property." *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- He suggested that we revise the chapter thoroughly before the test. → He said, "**Let's** revise the chapter thoroughly before the test." *(SSC 2025)*
- The officer ordered the fellow to be arrested. → The officer said, "**Arrest the fellow.**" *(CGL Mains 2019)*
- Calling me a friend, Surjit Singh encouraged me and advised me not to lose heart. → Surjit Singh **said**, "Cheer up, friend. Do not lose heart." *(SSC Steno 2024)*
- The old man prayed to God to help him in his hour of sorrow. → The old man said, "**O God, help me** in my hour of sorrow." *(CPO Mains 2023)*
- My son exclaimed with joy that it was an attractive site. → My son said, "**What an attractive site it is!**" *(CHSL Mains 2022)*
- They exclaimed sadly that India had lost the match in the World Cup finals. → They said, "**What a loss!** India has lost the match in the World Cup finals." *(CPO Mains 2024)*
- The teacher stated that water boils at 100 degrees Celsius. → "Water **boils** at 100 degrees Celsius," the teacher stated. *(SSC 2025)*
- The judge said that justice must be served at all costs. → "Justice **must be served** at all costs," the judge said. *(SSC 2025)*
- The CEO told the staff that deadlines must be followed strictly. → "Deadlines **must be followed** strictly," said the CEO. *(SSC 2025)*
- Mother said that when we pluck a flower it dies so we should let it beautify the world as long as it lives. → Mother said, "When we pluck a flower it **dies** so we should let it beautify the world as long as it **lives**." *(CGL Mains 2020)*
- I asked my friend how one could be merry when everything was so messed up. → I **said to** my friend, "How **can** one be merry when everything **is** so messed up?" † *(CPO Mains 2023)*

## 16. ⚠️ Top Traps

1. ❌ *asked that…* · ❌ *whether if…* — प्रश्न में `that` कभी नहीं, `if / whether` में से एक।
2. ❌ प्रश्न में **do / does / did** और `?` बचाना — Indirect में order सीधा होता है।
3. ❌ *suggested to + V1* — सही: **suggested that … should**।
4. ❌ *forbade … not to* — forbade / prohibited खुद negative हैं।
5. ❌ Universal truth में tense बदलना।
6. ❌ RV present / future हो तो tense / adverb बदलना — सिर्फ़ pronoun।
7. ❌ Time clause में **सिर्फ़ time clause** बदलना।
8. ❌ Interjection (Alas, Wow, Oh) छोड़ देना — इन्हें हटाकर **exclaimed with + भाव** लिखो।
9. ❌ Past perfect / other modals (must, might, could, would) पर फिर से backshift।
10. ❌ 'We' को हमेशा *they* बना देना — 4 केस देखो।
11. ❌ *told* बिना object के — **said to → told** (object ज़रूरी)।

## 17. ⚡ Last-Minute Revision Sheet

- **SON** → 1st = Subject · 2nd = Object · 3rd = No change
- RV **Past** → Pronoun + Tense + Adverb · RV **Present / Future** → सिर्फ़ Pronoun
- **एक कदम पीछे:** V1 → V2 · is/am/are → was/were · has/have → had · V2 → had + V3 · was/were → had been · will/shall → would · can → could · may → might
- No change: **Universal truth · Past perfect · other modals · Time clause (best) · Had/Were/Should inversion**
- **Adverb:** now→then · here→there · this→that · ago→before · today→that day · tomorrow→the next day · yesterday→the previous day · last→previous · next→following
- **Assertive** → that · **Interrogative** → if/whether या same WH-word + सीधा order · **Imperative** → to / not to + V1 · **Exclamatory** → exclaimed with … that · **Optative** → prayed / wished that … might
- **Let's** → suggested that … should · **Please** → requested · **Don't forget** → reminded · **Go on** → encouraged
- **What a…!** → that it was a… · **How + adj** → that … was very + adj · **Where…!** → nowhere
- **Thanks** → thanked · **Congratulations** → congratulated · **Good morning** → bade good morning · **Happy birthday** → wished
